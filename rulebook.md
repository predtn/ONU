# 🃏 SOUL! – Game Rulebook

---

## Overview

A color-matching card game for 2–6 players with a twist: losing isn't the end — it's the beginning of a challenge.

**Deck:** 68 cards across 4 colors — Red, Yellow, Green, Blue

---

## Card Types

### Number Cards (40 cards)
- Numbers **0–9**, one per color × 4 colors

### Action Cards (20 cards)

| Card | Effect |
|------|--------|
| **Skip** | Next player loses their turn |
| **Reverse** | Reverses play direction |
| **Draw 2** | Next player draws 2 cards and loses their turn |
| **Wild** | Change the active color |
| **Wild +4** | Change color + next player draws 4 and loses their turn |

> **Wild** and **Wild +4** are dual-function cards — they also carry a **Phase Challenge ability** printed on the card face (see below).

---

## Setup

1. Deal **7 cards** to each player.
2. Place the remaining cards face-down as the **Draw Pile**.
3. Flip the top card to start the **Play Pile**.

---

## On Your Turn

Play **1 valid card** matching the top card by:
- Same **color**
- Same **number**
- Same **action type**
- Any **Wild** or **Wild +4**

If you can't play → **draw 1 card** from the Draw Pile.

---

## YOUR SOUL IS MINE!

When you have **1 card left**, shout **"SOUL!"**

Forget to call it → **draw 2 cards as penalty.**

---

## Winning a Round

- **First player** to play all their cards **wins the round.**
- The player with the **most cards remaining** draws from the **Challenge Deck.**

---

## Dual-Function Special Cards

Wild and Wild +4 cards each carry a **hidden Phase Challenge ability**, printed on the card. These activate when a round ends.

| Card | Normal Play | Phase Challenge (on loss) |
|------|-------------|--------------------------|
| Wild | Change color | Trigger special ability |
| Wild +4 | Change color + Draw 4 | Trigger special ability |

**Strategy:** Hold these cards to flip the outcome after a round — or burn them to secure a win.

### Special Abilities

| Ability | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **Give Me a Chance** | Cancel the challenge — draw a new one instead |
| **Oh Come On!** | Cancel the challenge — but pay a price |
| **Lucky Guy** | Nullify the challenge entirely *(Phase Challenge only)* |
| **That's Rough, Buddy** | Redirect the penalty to another player |
| **I'm the King Now** | Redirect the penalty to the **round winner** |

**Card ratio:** 3 : 2 : 1 : 1 : 1 across Wild / Wild +4 cards.

---

## Challenge Deck

Drawn by the round's loser. Challenges come in 3 levels:

| Level | Count | Example |
|-------|-------|---------|
| **Level 1** – Light | 12 | Share a secret, compliment someone, pose for 5s |
| **Level 2** – Medium | — | *(see Challenge Deck)* |
| **Level 3** – Wild | — | *(see Challenge Deck)* |
